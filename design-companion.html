🎓 Tutoring Website Design - Visual Companion

Approach 1: Clean Academic + Code Focus

Hero with code snippet background + your profile photo. Dark theme (blues/grays) with accent highlights. Section-based layout emphasizing credentials and course curriculum.

Strengths

  • Instantly communicates "technical expert"
  • Code visuals resonate with students
  • High credibility through credentials display
  • Easy to implement & Vercel-friendly
  • Clean, focused, no distraction

Trade-offs

  • Might feel cold to younger high schoolers
  • Requires good typography/spacing

Approach 2: Credentials-First Showcase

Large hero showing your AXONS, Super AI, Hylife logos + awards section. Portrait photo prominent. Emphasis on "Taught by..." credibility. Course cards secondary.

Strengths

  • Maximum social proof upfront
  • Impressive for career-changers
  • Builds immediate trust
  • Great for LinkedIn sharing

Trade-offs

  • Takes longer to show course value
  • Logo-heavy might feel cluttered
  • Less focus on course details

Approach 3: Student-Focused Curriculum

Course cards are hero. Clean grid layout, each course shows: title, price, hours, what you'll build/learn. You appear in testimonial/about section lower down.

Strengths

  • Fastest path to course selection
  • High conversion for "just browsing"
  • Clear pricing/commitment upfront
  • Good for comparison shopping

Trade-offs

  • You feel less like the hero
  • First-time visitors don't know who you are
  • Less emotional connection
